About Me

I am a prospective Ph.D. applicant in Statistics and Machine Learning. I am completing my M.S. in Statistics at Hajee Mohammad Danesh Science and Technology University (HSTU), Dinajpur (expected December 2026). My M.S. thesis, Customer-Complaint Drivers in Mobile Financial Services, develops an explainable ML framework that analyses app reviews to reveal which customer emotions drive complaints, switching and fraud reports. I received my B.Sc. (Hons) in Statistics from HSTU in 2025.

My research sits where statistical modelling, machine learning and explainable AI meet real decisions. I am interested in models that are accurate, interpretable and trustworthy enough to act on: in business (customer complaints, employee attrition, retention decisions), science and engineering (near-Earth object classification, energy forecasting, software repositories), public health and the environment. My work combines classical statistics and survival analysis with deep learning and explanation methods such as SHAP and LIME.

I work as a Research Assistant at CeMRD. I also founded Uexcel, where I teach Bangladeshi university students research methods and data analysis with SPSS, Python and R; it has trained 1,700+ learners for free through live classes.

Research Interests

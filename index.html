About Me

I am an MS student in Statistics at HSTU, Dinajpur, where my thesis focuses on HR analytics and employee attrition. Since January 2023 I have worked remotely as a Research Assistant and Analytics Team Lead at CEMRD.

My research interests include explainable machine learning (SHAP, LIME, PDP), federated learning for environmental and tabular data, survival analysis in public health, causal and fairness-aware decision-making, and text and emotion analytics for Bangladeshi digital services.

I also founded Uexcel, a platform that teaches Bangladeshi university students research paper writing and data analysis with SPSS, Python and R. Uexcel has trained 1,700+ learners for free through live classes.

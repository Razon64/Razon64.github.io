About Me

I am a prospective Ph.D. applicant in Statistics and Machine Learning. I am completing my M.S. in Statistics at Hajee Mohammad Danesh Science and Technology University (HSTU), Dinajpur (expected December 2026), with a thesis on HR analytics and employee attrition. I received my B.Sc. (Hons) in Statistics from HSTU in 2025.

My research sits where statistical modelling, machine learning and explainable AI meet real decisions. I am interested in models that are accurate, interpretable and trustworthy enough to act on: in business (employee attrition, customer complaints, retention budgets), public health (survival analysis, clinical phenotyping) and the environment (air quality, heatwaves). My work combines classical statistics with deep learning, federated learning and explanation methods such as SHAP, LIME and partial dependence.

Since 2023 I have worked as a Research Assistant and Analytics Team Lead at CEMRD (remote). I also founded Uexcel, where I teach Bangladeshi university students research methods and data analysis with SPSS, Python and R; it has trained 1,700+ learners for free through live classes.
